# Book 1 of The Iliad

We open with the the goddess telling us of Achilles anger over the pains put upon Achaians, their death and destruction.

Chryseus, a priest of Apollo, pleads to the army to return his daughter in
exchange for a ransom. Agamemnon refuses.

Chryseus prays to Apollo who rains pestilence and fire onto the Achaian army.

Achilles tries to reason with Agamemnon. He refuses.

Achilles anger overcomes him but Athena intervenes for Hera's love of both of
them.

Achilles lashes out in more anger and threatens to leave the army to their ruin. 

Nestor, an elder man, attempts to find reason between both of them, to no avail.

They go their seperate ways, Agememnon commands Odysseus to take Chryseis to the
boat.

Agamemnon calls Taltyhbius and Eurybates to go to Achilles lodge and to lead
Briseis (his captive prize) away from him.

Achilles sees them coming but does not fight them. He orders Patroclus to
retrieve the girl for them but bearing witness his loss and to remember.

Achilles weeps to his mother in prayer who hears him. He recounts all that has
happened to him. He asks her to plead his case to Zeus and to help the Trojans
against Agamemnon.

Thetis regrets bearing him to such a short life. She grants his request and
tells him to stay clear of the fighting.

Odysseus returns Chryseis to her father Chryses and to perform a grand sacrifice
for Apollo. They feasted and drank to the god's honor.

Thetis begs Zeus to honor Achilles by granting favor to the Trojans until her
son's honor is repaid. Zeus is reluctant as it will drive war with Hera but he
agrees.

Zeus returns to Mt Olympus but Hera knows of his plans and taunts him into
telling her. After some back and forth, he reminds her there is nothing she can
do as she is powerless against his hands throttling her.

Hera is afraid but her child Hephaestus tries to comfort her. The immortals
laughed and feasted, with Zeus retiring to his bed and Hera by his side.
